# Treyarch and developer comments relevant to Revelations Rev-7

**Finding:** I located genuine comments about Revelations' ciphers as a group and a 2026 reaction to their solutions. I did **not** locate a verified statement specifically identifying Rev-7 / Origins Trench, or an official key, algorithm, plaintext clue or error correction for it. This is a bounded search result, not proof that no deleted or unindexed remark exists.

## Directly relevant interviews

### August 1, 2016 — MrDalekJD, before Revelations

At 15:09–15:46, the interviewer asks whether the unsolved Mob of the Dead ADFGX cipher uses double encryption. Blundell refuses to disclose that. The next exchange turns to DLC 4: he warns of greater difficulty and entertains the possibility that none of its ciphers will be solved. He does not identify an individual paper, key, layer count or method. [Original interview, 15:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QtGyU727Byw&t=909s).

The early affirmative-sounding answer in this exchange is explicitly clarified as a response about the key square, not confirmation of double encryption. It should not be cited as technical confirmation about Rev-7.

### June 8, 2017 — MrDalekJD, Zombies Chronicles interview

The 6:16–7:31 discussion directly concerns Revelations' ending and its remaining ciphers. Blundell acknowledges that they have not all been solved, says some were deliberately difficult, and jokes about receiving a solver's résumé. His remark about further things appearing supplies no specified mechanism or plaintext. [Original interview, 6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yc8pH-th27o&t=376s).

A separate discussion begins around 11:08 about the hidden, supposedly unsolvable Easter egg. The eight-year/vault remark occurs around 14:01–14:35, inside that later conversation. Treating it as a promise to reveal Rev-7 is unsupported. [Separate passage, 11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yc8pH-th27o&t=668s).

### August 17, 2018 — GameSpot interview with Blundell and Craig Houston

Houston jokes about overly difficult ciphers. Blundell then explains the design decision: late in BO3, harder Easter eggs and more ciphers were used to slow the release of story information. This is the clearest direct explanation of the difficulty policy, but it does not establish Rev-7's construction or integrity. [Original written interview](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/the-madmen-behind-call-of-dutys-zombies-tease-blac/1100-6461093/).

### March 25, 2026 — JCbackfire, “We're Not Done Yet.”

At 49:51–51:22, the conversation congratulates the recent solvers, mentions Randomiser and others, and asks how many Revelations ciphers remain and where the two remaining ones are located. No usable decoding instruction follows in the inspected exchange. [Original stream, 49:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r7FDjnn5JVg&t=2991s).

At 1:57:01–1:57:33, a separate John Dee question prompts a Francis Bacon reference. There is no explicit connection to Origins Trench. Nearby earlier references to reversing/inverting controls concern gameplay, not a cipher instruction. [John Dee passage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r7FDjnn5JVg&t=7021s).

These are later comments by a former Treyarch developer, not a 2026 statement issued by Treyarch Studios.

## Official social-media material

On January 15, 2019, Treyarch's verified account posted a video answering Richkiller's question about Blundell's favorite unsolved Zombies cipher. The original post remains accessible. [Official Treyarch post](https://x.com/Treyarch/status/1085322955976929280).

Charlie INTEL's contemporary account identifies his answer as Voyage of Despair and records a rumble reference. This is a real social-media cipher clue, but for another map. It does not justify treating controller vibration as part of Rev-7. [Contemporary answer transcription](https://www.charlieintel.com/news/115-day-jason-blundell-answers-audience-questions-on-twitter-50599/).

Targeted indexed searches of Twitter/X, Facebook and Instagram did not yield a post specifically identifying this paper. That is not a complete audit of those accounts' historical timelines.

## Supporting sources and qualifications

- **COD XP 2016:** An attendee's September 3 report of the September 2 panel says Blundell expected a difficult cipher could take two years and emphasized the intellectual challenge. The report does not securely identify the individual cipher. I would not apply that estimate specifically to Rev-7. [Contemporaneous panel report](https://www.newgamenetwork.com/article/1526/zombies-universe-panel-recap-cod-xp-2016/).
- **Activision, July 19, 2018:** Blundell discusses his programming background, pleasure in puzzles, and group problem-solving. Useful background, without a specific decoding clue. - **Jeff Gerstmann, March 17, 2025, 38:35–40:00:** Blundell describes bringing encrypted messages into Zombies and expresses a wish that some secrets remain undiscovered beyond his lifetime. He does not identify Rev-7. [Original podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LY37_Cenui4&t=2315s).
- **GodisaGeek, April 22, 2016:** The original interview describes community texture/audio inspection and collaborative solving, without a clue to this paper. [Original interview](https://godisageek.com/2016/04/treyarch-jason-blundell-talks-call-of-duty-zombies/).

## What changes our approach?

The findings support intentional difficulty and Blundell's continuing awareness of the solving effort. They do not establish that this paper is typo-free, that it uses a particular cipher, that a keyword is the sole missing piece, or that it is the Impossible Easter Egg.

The August 2016 refusal is not a confirmed layer count. The 2017 vault remark is not a Rev-7 release deadline. The 2019 rumble clue concerns Voyage. The 2025 wish about surviving secrets is not an identification of this ciphertext. The 2026 Bacon reference is not an authenticated Rev-7 key.

I found **context and attribution, but no new technical constraint strong enough to change the current Rev-7 attack**. The most relevant source for any future researcher to inspect is the March 25, 2026 exchange where the remaining locations are requested, followed by the direct 2017 discussion.

## Evidence quality and audit trail

Primary video metadata and public transcripts were checked for the relevant passages; these are automatic captions and were not independently audio-transcribed. The official social post was read directly; its spoken answer is supported by contemporary reporting. The panel's two-year estimate remains an attendee paraphrase.

The search covered indexed public sources and identified recordings, not every unindexed/deleted social post or every hour of developer appearances. One search result made a 2016 MrRoflWaffles interview appear recent; checking the original upload date prevented treating it as 2026 evidence.
